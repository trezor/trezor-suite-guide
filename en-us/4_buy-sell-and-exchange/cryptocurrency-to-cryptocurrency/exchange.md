# Swap

1. Select **Swap** in the left menu bar.
2. Choose the cryptocurrency you wish to swap and enter the **Swap amount.**
3. Select the cryptocurrency you want to receive; you can pick from any asset that Trezor supports.
4. Select a **Provider** for the Swap.
5. Once you have selected your preferred offer, click **Swap.**
6. Set a **Fee**. This is a network fee for the transaction to be carried out.
7. After you've reviewed the Swap transaction details, click **Confirm on Trezor & send.**
8. Confirm the transaction details and sign it **using your Trezor.**
9. Then click **Swap.**
10. Wait for the transaction to be finalized.

> 💡 Learn more about [swapping crypto in Trezor Suite](https://trezor.io/guides/sending-receiving-staking-funds/trading-crypto-in-trezor-suite/swap-crypto-in-trezor-suite) on the Trezor knowledge base
