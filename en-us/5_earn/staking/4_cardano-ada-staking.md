# Cardano (ADA) staking

Staking ADA means putting your ADA behind a staking pool and earning a share of the rewards the pool receives. Your ADA stays in your account the whole time and you can use it anytime. In Trezor Suite, you stake with a pool run by Everstake.

You can start staking ADA from the **Earn** dashboard in Trezor Suite.

#### Why stake ADA

- **Earn rewards** – staked ADA earns rewards over time.
- **Support the network** – staked ADA helps secure Cardano.
- **No technical setup** – Everstake runs the staking pool.
- **Confirm on your Trezor** – every staking action is confirmed on your device.

#### Key points

- **Your ADA is not locked** – you can send or trade your ADA at any time while staking.
- **Whole balance** – your entire account balance is staked, including ADA you receive later.
- **Deposit** – a refundable deposit of 2 ADA is required to start staking. You get it back when you unstake.
- **Automatic compounding** – rewards are added to your staked balance.
- **Supported devices** – available on Trezor Model T, Safe 3, Safe 5 and Safe 7.
