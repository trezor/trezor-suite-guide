# Ethereum yield

Ethereum yield means depositing your ETH into a vault that lends it to borrowers. The interest the borrowers pay is your yield. It's an alternative to staking – your ETH is lent out rather than staked, and you can take it back at any time. In Trezor Suite, the vault is run by Morpho.

You can start earning yield from the **Earn** dashboard in Trezor Suite.

#### Why earn yield on ETH

- **Earn yield** – your deposited ETH earns yield over time.
- **No waiting** – unlike staking, there is no queue to enter or leave. You can withdraw at any time.
- **No technical setup** – Morpho runs the vault.
- **Confirm on your Trezor** – every deposit and withdrawal is confirmed on your device.

#### Key points

- **Wrapped ETH** – the vault accepts ETH in its wrapped form, called WETH. You can wrap your ETH during the deposit and unwrap it again when you withdraw.
- **Automatic compounding** – yield is added to your deposit.
- **Withdraw any time** – your deposit is never locked, and withdrawals are instant.
- **Vault token** – after you deposit, a token representing your deposit appears in your account. It is redeemed when you withdraw.
- **Supported devices** – available on all Trezor devices with up-to-date firmware.
