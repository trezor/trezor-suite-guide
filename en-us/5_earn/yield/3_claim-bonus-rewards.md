# Claim bonus rewards

Bonus rewards are extra rewards that a vault may offer on top of its regular yield. They are paid in a different token than the one you deposited – for Morpho vaults, in MORPHO tokens – and they are not added to your deposit automatically. You collect them by claiming them.

When you have bonus rewards to claim, a **Claim** button appears in the **Earn** dashboard in Trezor Suite.

Bonus campaigns run only from time to time and for a limited period, so there may be nothing to claim. Rewards you earned during a campaign stay claimable after it ends.
