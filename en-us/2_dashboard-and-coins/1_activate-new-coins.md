# Activate new networks

On the Trezor Suite **Dashboard**, click the **'+Add networks'** button in the **My assets** overview section.

You will then be taken directly to the **Add your networks** settings menu:

* Enable / disable a network using the toggle switch.
* A green toggle indicates that particular network is enabled in Suite.

The **Networks** settings menu can also be accessed by clicking on **Settings (⚙️)** in the top-right corner of Suite, and then selecting the **Networks** tab.

#### **ERC20 tokens**

Ethereum network ERC20 tokens can be managed using Trezor Suite by adding custom token addresses to a new or existing Ethereum Account.

> 💡 Learn more about [supported cryptocurrencies](https://trezor.io/learn/supported-assets/supported-coins) on the Trezor knowledge base
